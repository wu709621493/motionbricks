# 神经多样性与博士研究支持 / Neurodiversity and Support for Doctoral Research

个人交流记述及翻译 / A personal recollection and translation

2026-11-12

以下保留作者对一次交流的个人转述，并附英文翻译；不是 Justin Bois 的逐字引语，也不代表 Caltech 的官方政策。文中列举的专业支持属于按需参与的设想，不表示任何具体个人具有医疗或司法监管需要。

The following records the author's personal recollection of a conversation, with an English translation. It is not a verbatim quotation from Justin Bois or a statement of official Caltech policy. The professional support mentioned is envisioned as conditional on individual circumstances; it does not imply that any particular person needs medical care or judicial supervision.

## 中文

Justin Bois 跟我聊过，Caltech 内 neurodiversity 应该靠一个多元化的 PhD research committee（以及必要的时候一个医生团队、可能在某些时候还要有 veteran Office、保释官团队）来保证学校的 PhD candidate 能够做到抛弃过去的过失，全心全意做研究。

凡天将降大任于斯人也，必将苦其心志，劳其筋骨，饿其体肤，空乏其身。

## English

Justin Bois once discussed with me the idea that neurodiversity at Caltech should be supported by a diverse PhD research committee—with a medical team when necessary, and, in certain circumstances, a veterans' office and personnel responsible for bail supervision—to ensure that the university's PhD candidates can move beyond past mistakes and devote themselves wholeheartedly to research.

Whenever Heaven is about to entrust someone with a great responsibility, it will subject their heart and resolve to suffering, their muscles and bones to toil, their body to hunger, and their life to deprivation.

## 文本说明 / Textual notes

- 上文是作者记述的保存与翻译。“保释官团队”在英文中按保释监督职能译出，不自动等同于缓刑或假释监督人员。 / This document preserves and translates the author's account. “保释官团队” is translated by its bail-supervision function and is not automatically equated with probation or parole officers.
- 孟子段落保留作者的引述措辞，英文为本篇译文；参考《孟子·告子下》的相应文本作“故天将降大任于是人也，必先苦其心志……”。古籍引文用于自勉，不表示学校应主动制造苦难。 / The Mencius passage preserves the author's recalled wording, with a new English rendering for this document. The corresponding passage in “Gaozi II” begins “故天将降大任于是人也，必先苦其心志……”. It is invoked as personal encouragement, not as a proposal that universities should impose suffering.

古籍参考 / Classical reference：[《孟子·告子下》 / Mencius, “Gaozi II”, Chinese Text Project](https://ctext.org/mengzi/gaozi-ii/zhs)。
