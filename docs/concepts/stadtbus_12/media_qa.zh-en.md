# Media Q&A / 媒体问答
**Independent concept / 独立概念 · v0.1 · 2026-11-17**

## Is this an official Porsche launch? / 这是保时捷官方发布吗？

No. It is an independent design study by Mengcheng Wu in collaboration with ChatGPT. The working name explains the design question. There is no Porsche endorsement, commercial partnership or production commitment. “First bus” describes the hypothetical programme, not a verified historical first.

不是。这是吴孟城与 ChatGPT 协作整理的独立设计研究。“第一台巴士”是设想中的项目定位，不是对汽车史的已核实首创声明。

## What is the essential design signature? / 最重要的标志细节是什么？

A physical start control to the driver's left, retained for both driving layouts and any future powertrain derivative. The control issues a request; the electric vehicle must show its actual readiness separately.

驾驶席左侧的实体启动控制；左右舵及后续不同动力版本一律保留。启动请求与实际就绪状态分别表达。

## Does left-hand start prove the bus is safer? / 左手启动证明更安全吗？

No safety benefit has been measured. It is the author's fixed identity and interaction requirement. Reach, mistaken operation, egress and state feedback still require validation.

尚无已测得的安全优势。这是作者确定的身份与交互要求，触及、误操作、疏散及状态反馈仍须验证。

## Is this simply a sports-car chassis made longer? / 是把跑车底盘拉长吗？

The proposal calls for a bus-specific structure and commercial-duty components, with Porsche-inspired calibration priorities. Published Macan systems are engineering background, not proof of suitability for bus mass or service life.

提案要求客车专用结构和商用工况部件。Macan 的已公开系统仅作技术背景，不能证明其能直接承受巴士质量或营运寿命。

## What does the proposal learn from BYD? / 向比亚迪学什么？

BYD's B12.b product materials describe integrated battery packaging, thermal management and controlled damping. These are relevant precedents. The concept makes no independently tested superiority claim and announces no BYD supply agreement.

B12.b 官方资料介绍了电池集成、热管理及可控阻尼，提供相关产品先例。本概念未作独立测试的优越性判断，也没有宣布比亚迪供货协议。

## What range, charging time or price is promised? / 承诺多少续航、充电时间与价格？

None. Capacity and charging choices depend on the route, climate, depot and operating reserve. About 30 seats and 70 total passengers are preliminary layout targets, not approved figures. No performance, saving or commercial availability claim is ready for publication.

均未承诺。约30座、总载客约70人也只是初步布置目标。性能、节省金额及供应时间尚无可公布的验证结果。

## What would demonstrate success? / 怎样证明成功？

A declared-baseline evaluation of comfort, emergency performance, accessibility, availability, energy use, repair work and cost per passenger-kilometre. Participants and load conditions must represent intended service. The validation matrix is a plan, and every test is currently NOT RUN.

以公开基线评价舒适、紧急性能、无障碍、出勤、耗电、维修和每客公里成本；样本与载荷须对应目标营运。矩阵仅是计划，目前全部未执行。

## What is available to journalists? / 媒体可以取得什么？

Bilingual text, the proposed stage script, engineering requirements, source notes and the author quotation. There are no prototype photos, measured charts, partner quotations or press contacts authorised by a manufacturer in this package. Any future visuals need honest concept labels and appropriate usage rights.

本包提供双语文字、讲稿、工程要求、来源及作者原话；没有实车照片、实测曲线、合作方引言或厂方授权的媒体联系人。后续图片须标明概念身份并处理使用权。
