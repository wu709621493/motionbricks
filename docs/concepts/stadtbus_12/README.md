## The proposition / 核心命题

A bus should feel precise to its driver, composed to its passengers and practical to its operator.  
司机觉得好揸，阿婆觉得坐得稳，车厂觉得养得起。

**Start on the left. Carry everyone forward. / 左手启动，载大家向前。**

The left-hand-start rule applies to any powertrain derivative and both left- and right-hand-drive layouts. The first vehicle proposal is battery electric; combustion and hybrid variants are not active development programmes.
