# Stadtbus 12: precision for everyone on board
**Independent Porsche-inspired city-bus concept — presentation text, v0.1**  
17 November 2026 | Concept author: Mengcheng Wu (Windy)

**Start on the left. Carry everyone forward.**

Stadtbus 12 is an independent design proposal exploring how Porsche-inspired precision, material discipline and driver engagement could serve everyday public transport. The concept envisages a roughly twelve-metre, single-deck, battery-electric low-floor city bus, developed around three people: the driver completing a shift, the passenger finding their balance and the technician preparing tomorrow's service.

The proposal sets an initial layout target of approximately thirty seats and seventy passengers in total. These are design intentions, subject to accessible-layout design, passenger-mass assumptions, axle loads and approval. Battery capacity, motor rating and charging power would be selected after route, climate and depot analysis.

At the centre of the concept is a physical start control positioned to the driver's left. Author Mengcheng Wu's original requirement is direct: “不管系油系电，一定要左手打火” — whether combustion-powered or electric, start with the left hand. The placement is retained in both left- and right-hand-drive concepts.

For the electric proposal, a tactile rotary control initiates a start request. A distinct READY indication appears only when the required system checks and enabling conditions are satisfied. Starting does not select a drive direction or release the parking brake. Final control logic remains subject to vehicle-level safety engineering and human-factors validation.

The chassis proposal combines air suspension, electronically controlled damping and load-aware calibration. Development would focus on progressive acceleration, consistent blending of regenerative and friction braking, and controlled body motion throughout the cabin. Comfort would be evaluated at the rear seats and standing areas as well as at the driver's seat. Emergency braking and stability requirements take precedence over comfort objectives.

The proposed exterior brings together rounded front surfaces, defined lamp shoulders, a four-point lighting motif and clean side panels. A burgundy-and-ivory colour study accompanies a warm-grey interior, visible handholds, accessible stop controls and planned wheelchair and stroller accommodation.

Maintainability is part of the design brief: replaceable exterior sections, accessible service modules and an early relationship with an experienced bus manufacturer. Any such relationship would need to be established; none is announced.

The proposed development sequence begins with packaging and route feasibility, continues through hardware and human-factors evaluation, and would progress to a small pilot fleet only after the necessary engineering and approvals. Evaluation would cover complete seasonal operation, energy use, availability, maintenance hours and cost per passenger-kilometre with occupancy disclosed.

**Project status:** independent concept only. No production vehicle, performance result, commercial agreement, order book, launch venue, price or on-sale date is announced. This text is not issued or endorsed by Porsche AG.
