# Stage script / 舞台讲稿
**Independent concept / 独立概念 · v0.1 · 2026-11-17**

Use the English or Chinese track for the event; do not read both as one continuous speech. Timing must be established in rehearsal. Every screen carries “Independent concept / 独立概念”. Cues below describe proposed graphics or actions, not existing media or a functioning vehicle.

现场按语言选择一条讲稿；双语同传另行安排。所有动作仅为舞台创意。尚无车辆或控制样机，因此不能把提示灯、模拟动画称为实车演示。

## 1. Opening / 开场

[Screen: city route, a passenger holding a rail, a technician opening a service panel. Planned original footage or licensed material only.]

**EN:** Good evening. A bus brings many expectations into one vehicle. A driver wants clear responses. A passenger wants a steady place to stand. A technician wants access to the part that needs attention. An operator wants the vehicle ready for its next shift. Tonight, we present an independent design study built around those everyday expectations: Stadtbus 12.

**中：** 各位晚上好。一台巴士承载着许多人的要求。司机希望车辆反应清楚；乘客希望站得稳；维修师傅希望够得着需要处理的部件；营运公司希望下一班能够准时出车。今晚，我们提出一项围绕这些日常要求展开的独立设计研究：Stadtbus 12。

## 2. Reveal / 揭幕

[Screen: proposed silhouette, labelled “Concept illustration — vehicle not built”. No fabricated prototype photography.]

**EN:** Our starting point is a twelve-metre-class electric city bus with a low floor. The initial layout aims for around thirty seats and seventy people in total, subject to engineering and approval. We want the character of the vehicle to be felt through the quality of an ordinary journey: pulling away, taking a corner, crossing uneven road and stopping at the next platform.

**中：** 我们的起点是一台12米级、低地台纯电城市巴士。初步布置希望提供约30个座位、总载客约70人，最终须经工程核算及审批。我们希望人们在每一段普通旅程中感到车辆的性格：起步、过弯、经过不平的路面，再停靠下一站。

## 3. The signature / 标志细节

[Screen: left side of the driver's workstation; a hand turns an illustrated physical control.]

**EN:** The concept author, Mengcheng Wu, gave us one requirement that stays with the vehicle, whatever its powertrain: start with the left hand. The physical control sits to the driver's left in both driving layouts. On the electric concept, the turn requests startup. The vehicle checks its conditions. READY is shown only when those conditions are satisfied. Selecting a direction and releasing the parking brake remain separate actions.

Start on the left. Carry everyone forward.

**中：** 概念作者吴孟城提出了一条要求，不随动力形式改变：“不管系油系电，一定要左手打火。”无论左舵还是右舵，实体启动装置都位于驾驶席左侧。在纯电概念中，旋转意味着发出启动请求；车辆检查条件，满足条件才显示 READY。选择方向和释放驻车制动，仍然是独立动作。

左手启动，载大家向前。

## 4. Precision in daily service / 日常营运中的精准

[Screen: proposed acceleration and cabin-motion evaluation locations. No invented measurement curves.]

**EN:** Air suspension, controlled damping and load-aware calibration are proposed tools. Their value would need to be measured throughout the cabin. We would evaluate the final approach to a stop, the transition between braking systems and the motion felt by a passenger at the rear. Emergency braking and vehicle stability come first. Comfort is developed within that obligation.

**中：** 空气悬挂、可控阻尼与按载荷标定，是我们提出的工具。它们的价值，需要在整个车厢里测量。我们会评价进站最后一段的制动、不同制动系统之间的切换，以及后排乘客感受到的运动。紧急制动和整车稳定性优先，舒适性在这个前提下开发。

## 5. Service and the next step / 维修与下一步

[Screen: modular exterior panel, accessible passenger layout, seasonal pilot plan.]

**EN:** A panel should be replaceable. A stop button should be reachable. A wheelchair space should be planned from the beginning. Battery size should follow the route, including its weather and charging opportunities. These decisions belong in the first design review.

The next step would be a feasibility programme with an experienced bus manufacturer, followed by validation and a carefully scoped pilot. No partner or production programme is announced today.

Our ambition is simple: a driver who enjoys the precision, a passenger who trusts the ride and a maintenance team that can prepare the bus for tomorrow. Thank you.

**中：** 外饰板要方便更换，下车按钮要方便触及，轮椅空间要从第一张布置图开始考虑。电池大小要跟着线路走，把天气与充电机会一起算进去。这些决定，都应该进入第一次设计评审。

下一步建议与有经验的客车制造商开展可行性研究，再进行验证与范围明确的试点。今天没有宣布已签约伙伴或量产计划。

我们的目标很具体：司机享受精准，乘客信任这段旅程，维修团队能够为明日出车做好准备。谢谢。
